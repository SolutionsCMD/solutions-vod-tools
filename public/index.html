YouTube cookies (import)

For age-gated, members-only, or login-walled YouTube content. Export a cookies.txt from your real browser and import it here — yt-dlp uses it automatically for every download afterwards.

How to export:
  1. Install Get cookies.txt LOCALLY (Chrome / Edge / Brave) or cookies.txt (Firefox).
  2. Sign in to YouTube in that browser. For age-gated content, watch the video once to confirm the gate accepts your account.
  3. On a youtube.com tab, click the extension icon → Export. Save the .txt anywhere.
  4. Click Import cookies.txt below and pick the file.
